Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition Lab

Efficient Representation & Understanding of Visual Intelligence

The CVPR Lab researches computer vision and multimodal AI technologies for efficiently representing and understanding visual information in images and video, applying this work to video intelligence, intelligent surveillance, medical imaging, and industrial vision.

About the Lab

The CVPR Lab develops computer vision technologies that contribute to solving real-world problems by researching algorithms that interpret visual data accurately and efficiently. Our work spans from the fundamental theory of image and video recognition to the construction of applied systems, and we pursue the practical impact of our research through collaboration with industry and medical institutions.

02

Research Areas

Video Intelligence

Video understanding technologies that interpret and analyze actions and situations within video.

Intelligent Surveillance

Real-time video analysis for anomaly detection and intelligent surveillance systems.

Medical Imaging

Deep learning-based technologies for medical image interpretation and diagnostic assistance.

Industrial Vision

Automated inspection and recognition technologies for manufacturing and industrial settings.
